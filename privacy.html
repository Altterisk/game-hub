Privacy

Applies to hub.altterisk.cc and the game sites linked from it. Last updated 2 November 2026.

What the sites store

Settings and progress you enter, such as farm plans, collections and parties, are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Google Drive sync

Sync is optional and off until you sign in with Google. When you sign in, the site asks Google for two things:

  • Its own app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The site saves one file there with your saved progress, so another device signed in to the same account can load it. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, shown in the Sync menu so you can tell which account is connected.

This runs entirely in your browser, between your device and Google. No server of ours receives your data, your email or your Google sign-in. The sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage until it expires or you sign out.

Sign out from the Sync menu to stop syncing. To delete the synced file, open Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps, and choose “Delete hidden app data” for the app. You can also remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to sync your progress and is never shared, sold or used for advertising.

Hosting and third parties

The sites are hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which processes standard request data such as your IP address under Cloudflare's privacy policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts. Game art and audio on some sites load from the game's own servers. Donations through Ko-fi are handled by Ko-fi under its own policy.
